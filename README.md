# Factor Calculator

A fast, free online tool that finds the factors of any number — with prime factorization, greatest common factor (GCF), and least common multiple (LCM) included.

## What it does
- Lists all factors of any whole number instantly
- Shows prime factorization with a clear factor tree
- Computes GCF and LCM for two or more numbers
- No sign-up, no downloads — works right in the browser

## Who it's for
Students brushing up on arithmetic, teachers preparing classroom examples, and anyone who wants quick, reliable answers to everyday math questions.

## Try it
Use the [free online factor calculator](https://factorcalculator.org) — type any number and get the full factor breakdown in seconds.
